## Ownership scan

- Physical/DS4: unmodified raw values, report quantization is an explicit plant
  boundary; no input smoothing or new deadzone.
- Intent and D: the 25% semantic admission is retained. Investigate whether a
  binary D flag is incorrectly reused as actuator authority.
- Selection/ADS/lifecycle: protect target IDs, generation, LT token, fresh
  completion, handover and no-target passthrough with existing production tests.
- Response estimator: separate free/slow regions; manual/fire ambiguity stops
  learning. Its confidence is not independent game-plant truth.
- Motion observer: capture-aligned command plus screen velocity; inspect how
  response/delay mismatch contaminates the sustaining-motion estimate.
- Position/motion solver and dynamics: distinguish required moving-target
  feedforward from a residual generated by the controller itself.
- Final arbitration: shaping AI alone cannot constrain jumps introduced later
  by manual/AI ownership changes.
- AutoFire/recoil/output: independent owners; firing and recoil are not evidence
  of target movement. Preserve finite single final output and release behavior.
